## 💡 The Idea

Healthcare information is often scattered across paper files, phone galleries, emails, messaging apps, diagnostic centers, and hospital portals.

Finding the right prescription or remembering previous medical information shouldn't be unnecessarily difficult.

**CareVault** is an AI-powered healthcare records platform designed to help people organize, access, and understand their medical history.

The vision is simple:

> **Your Health. Your History. One Place.**

One platform to bring your medical records together and make your healthcare journey easier to manage.
